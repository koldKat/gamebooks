Gamebook Tracker

Gamebook Tracker is a free, browser-based tool for mapping and tracking playthroughs of section-numbered gamebooks - Fighting Fantasy, Choose Your Own Adventure, Lone Wolf, Sorcery!, Fabled Lands, Grailquest, DestinyQuest, Tunnels & Trolls, and any other series that uses numbered sections. It builds a live, force-directed graph of every section you visit, showing choices, outcomes, dead ends, and alternate routes across the whole book.

Record every choice as you play, and revisit any completed or in-progress run to see its path highlighted on the map. Each book supports unlimited playthroughs, so you can compare how different runs branched from the same sections.

Beyond mapping, it includes custom character sheets with editable stat fields per book, an item inventory with a shared icon library, a paper-doll equipment panel, battle simulators with automated dice rolls and round-by-round combat logs, notebook clues and priority markings, and open world series support for linking multiple books into one continuous adventure with a character that carries over between them.

It's completely free - no subscriptions, no ads, no premium tiers. Supports multiple users and multiple books per account, with public profiles and a shared activity feed for anyone who wants to show off a run.

Interactive adventure tracking for classic gamebooks

Map every path. Track every run. Revisit every ending.

Gamebook Tracker helps you build a living record of your interactive adventure books. Follow branching routes section by section, keep multiple playthroughs, mark visited and discovered passages, organize books, anthologies, series, and stashes, and return later to see exactly how each choice changed the journey.

Whether you are replaying Fighting Fantasy, cataloguing magazine anthologies, or mapping your own growing library of classic gamebooks, the tracker turns every book into a searchable history of routes, wins, deaths, notes, and endings.

Battle Simulators
Run combat encounters with automated dice rolls, hit resolution, and round-by-round logs that follow each book's specific rules.
Play Together
Form a party with friends and move through a book side by side - the same run, synced live, so nobody's stuck reading ahead alone.
Community & Forum
See what other players are up to in a live activity feed, rate books, and swap tips or simulator requests on the forum.
Open World Series
Link books into a living world. One character, one run number, many books - portal nodes carry your adventure across the entire series so each volume is a chapter in a single journey, not an island.
